* not really my problem kinda, but people do have an interesting psychological bias towards points
    * there might be a course that's a really good fit for them, but they see it's low points, and they associate that with the course being "stupid" or "too easy"
    * also, there might be some people that don't demonstrate interest in an area (e.g. law), but then when they find out it's prestigious or that it's high points, they might become interested in it
    * in light of this, you could consider hiding the points in the RS?
    * but I don't think this is a feasible solution because the user is one google search from finding out the points anyway, and you don't want to "trick" a person into choosing a course
